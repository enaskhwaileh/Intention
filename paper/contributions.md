# Contribution candidates

These are hypotheses for paper positioning, not accepted novelty claims.

1. A formal separation between topical need and intended analytical action for
   single-table discovery.
2. An explicit alignment contract between `Psi*(I)` action requirements and
   `Phi*(T)` observable table capabilities.
3. Evidence-aware feasibility and inspectable provenance traces that separate
   missing from contradictory evidence.
4. A reproducible action-switch evaluation protocol, contingent on benchmark
   construction and independent judgments.

The generic claim that this is the "first work" is unsupported and must not be
used. Contributions require direct comparison with FollowTable, KATS,
DataScout, and other verified related work.
